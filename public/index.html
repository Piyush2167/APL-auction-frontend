Live sync across every screen

The server records each bid first, then sends it to captains, the admin desk and the public screen together. A dropped connection catches up on its own.

Questions

What captains and viewers ask before the auction. See the mystery players

? Mystery playerBowler · swing · base 20 pts Use token, 1 left

Who can bid in the auction?

Only team captains, signed in from the admin's email invite.

What if two captains bid on a player at once?

The first one the server records wins. The other captain can bid again.

What is a mystery player?

A player whose name is hidden. A team can spend its token to see it.

Can a team sell a player back?

Yes. Once the admin approves a resale request, the price returns to the purse.
